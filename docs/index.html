Parking+

Parking, Salik and fine discount reminders for UAE drivers. Free, no account, your data stays on your phone.

Help

Parking reminder doesn't appear. Allow notifications, and set location to "Always" (iPhone) or "Allow all the time" (Android). On Android, also set Parking+ to "Unrestricted" in battery settings. The reminder only comes during the emirate's paid hours, and not at places you saved as free spots.

Salik balance is wrong. The balance is an estimate. Check it in the Salik app and correct it on the Salik screen.

Parking SMS failed. Check the message against the parking sign before sending, and always wait for the confirmation SMS from the parking authority. If no confirmation arrives, you have not paid.

Discount campaigns. Parking+ lists police fine discount campaigns and reminds you before they end. Always read the official announcement before paying.

الدعم

تطبيق مواقف+: تذكيرات المواقف وسالك وتخفيضات المخالفات لسائقي الإمارات. مجاني، بدون حساب، وبياناتك تبقى على هاتفك.

لا يظهر تذكير الموقف: اسمح بالإشعارات واجعل الموقع "دائمًا" (آيفون) أو "السماح طوال الوقت" (أندرويد)، وفي أندرويد اجعل التطبيق "غير مقيد" في إعدادات البطارية.

رصيد سالك غير صحيح: الرصيد تقديري. تحقق منه في تطبيق سالك وصححه في شاشة سالك.
