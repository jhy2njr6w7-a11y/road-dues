15. ~~Parking reminder fires while driving~~ **built in PR #29 (branch `claude/park-detect`), waiting for "merge it" + build 5 drive test.** Details:
    **Bug: "Did you pay?" fires at any stop, even traffic lights / traffic** (Saif, 29 Oct 2026). Cause: only GPS updates with speed ≥ 6 m/s (22 km/h) re-arm the 4-min timer, so crawling traffic or a long red light counts as parked. Planned fix (no new native modules):
    - count slow movement (≥ ~2 m/s) as still driving, so traffic doesn't trigger it;
    - **"walked away" signal:** after the car stops, if the next GPS updates move away at walking speed (~1–2 m/s, > 40 m from where the car stopped), the user parked and left the car → notify right away;
    - if no walking is seen, wait longer (e.g. 8–10 min stopped) before asking;
    - a "Not a parking spot" / "Don't ask here" button on the notification that saves the place (like free spots);
    - **Update 29 Oct:** Saif says it fires even after stopping for only a few seconds. So the 4-min timer is firing while he drives: the background task is not getting (or not finishing) GPS updates often enough to keep pushing it back (possible causes: iOS delivering updates late/in batches in the background, `speed` reported as -1 so updates aren't counted as moving, the task running out of background time, e.g. while `refreshCampaigns()` downloads). Fix must not rely on the timer alone: re-arm on any update that moved > 50 m from the last stop point (whatever `speed` says), move the campaign download after the parking logic, use the walked-away signal as the main trigger and a longer fallback (10 min).
    - **Saif's finding (29 Oct):** it happens after a short stop (~1 min at a light) and then driving on: the reminder still arrives ~4 min later while he is driving. So after a stop the app does not notice it moved again (updates after the pause aren't delivered/handled in time). Planned extra fix: when the car stops, add a **geofence (~100 m) around the stop point** (`Location.startGeofencingAsync`); iOS wakes the app reliably on leaving it even when suspended → cancel the reminder. Plus handle the parking logic before the campaign download.
    - **Walking vs driving (Saif asked):** iPhone's motion chip knows. Use `expo-sensors` Pedometer (iOS CMPedometer): `getStepCountAsync(stopTime, now)` gives steps since the car stopped, even if the app was asleep. Steps > ~20 after the stop = he got out and walked → ask "Did you pay?". Needs the "Motion & Fitness" permission (NSMotionUsageDescription). Combine with GPS speed (walking ~1–2 m/s vs driving). Android has no step history API in Expo, so keep the GPS method there. Install with the SDK's exact version (see iOS launch crash note).
    - **Parked but stays in the car (Saif asked):** still ask, via the fallback: no steps and still inside the stop geofence after ~10 min → "Did you pay?". Walking → ask at once. Let the user pick the in-car wait in Settings (5 / 10 / 15 min).
    - add a hidden **trip log** (Settings ▸ tap version 5×) that records each GPS update time/speed and each schedule/cancel, so Saif can screenshot it after a drive and we see exactly why it fired.
